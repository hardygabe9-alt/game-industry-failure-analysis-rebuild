# Annual Game Sales Research Log

## Research standard
- Period: 2008–2026
- Geography: Global
- Primary metric: Annual dollar sales
- Coverage: Broad list of commercially significant games
- 2026: Provisional; incomplete calendar year

## Evidence rules
1. Record the exact source and publication date.
2. Record whether the source covers PC, console, mobile, or multiple platforms.
3. Distinguish games released during the year from all games earning revenue during the year.
4. Do not substitute lifetime copies sold for annual revenue.
5. Do not substitute regional rankings for global rankings.
6. Mark estimates and publisher-reported figures clearly.
7. Leave unsupported values blank.
